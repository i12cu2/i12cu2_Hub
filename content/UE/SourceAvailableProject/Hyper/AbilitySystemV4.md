Hyper Ability System v4 gives you a complete framework to build dynamic abilities, effects, and buffs for your Unreal Engine 5 project. It is an independent solution built fully in Blueprints, made for developers who prefer a simpler, more direct approach without the complexity of GAS. Hyper v3 modules are built to fit together perfectly, so you can grow your game from a small project into a full experience without rework.
