文档与教程

✅ 主要特性  
单组件集成：通过一个组件即可将燃油管理系统添加到您的基础 Chaos Vehicle 蓝图中。

燃油消耗与油箱管理：通过曲线浮点器配置当前燃油量、最大容量、储备油量、消耗倍率以及基于转速的燃油消耗。

多人游戏支持：完全复制的局域网和互联网多人模式，支持监听服务器和专用服务器。

两种加油系统：可使用角色与油管、便携油桶进行交互式操作，或使用简单的车辆加油点实现渐进式或即时加注。

燃油状态小部件：显示油箱百分比、油耗（升/百公里或英里/加仑）以及剩余续航里程（公里或英里）。

交互式加油机：通过可配置的加油泵小部件追踪已加注的升数或加仑数，并计算总燃油费用。

加油站管理器：配置燃油价格，并自动设置场景中连接的加油泵。

便携油桶与油管：包含可交互的对象系统，支持携带式储油容器和抽油软管。

角色交互与反向动力学（IK）：抓取、放下及交互动画采用IK和链接动画图，便于与您自定义的角色集成。

兼容Chaos Vehicles：支持任意Unreal Engine Chaos Vehicle，并可直接与Digital Dive Vehicles集成。

Complete Multiplayer Vehicle Fuel System for Unreal Engine 5
Advanced Fuel System is a complete UE5 vehicle fuel and refueling system for any Chaos Vehicle, adding fuel consumption, tank management, interactive gas stations, fuel pumps, jerry cans and full multiplayer support.

Built entirely with Blueprints, it integrates through a single component and supports both character-based refueling and simple vehicle refuel points—without requiring C++ coding or third-party plugins.

Documentation & Tutorials

✅ Key Features
One-component integration: Add fuel management to your base Chaos Vehicle Blueprint through a single component.

Fuel consumption & tank management: Configure current fuel, maximum capacity, reserve, consumption multiplier and RPM-based fuel consumption through a Curve Float.

Multiplayer: Fully replicated for LAN and Internet multiplayer, with listen and dedicated server support.

Two refueling systems: Use character-based interaction with fuel hoses and jerry cans, or simple vehicle refuel points for gradual or instant refilling.

Fuel status widget: Display tank percentage, fuel consumption in L/100 km or MPG, and estimated range in kilometers or miles.

Interactive fuel pumps: Track liters or gallons dispensed and total fuel price through configurable pump widgets.

Gas Station Manager: Configure fuel pricing and automatically set up connected fuel pumps in the scene.

Jerry cans & fuel hoses: Includes an interactable object system with portable fuel containers and pump hoses.

Character interaction & IK: Grab, drop and interact animations use IK and Linked Anim Graphs for easier integration with your own characters.

Chaos Vehicles compatibility: Works with any Unreal Engine Chaos Vehicle and integrates directly with Digital Dive Vehicles.

The complete Advanced Fuel System Blueprint framework, including a sample vehicle based on Unreal Engine’s Chaos Vehicle, two example gas stations, basic and advanced refueling interactions, fuel pumps, hoses, jerry cans, widgets and the Gas Station Manager.
