✅ 主要特性  
完整的角色互动：驾驶员和乘客可通过自适应上下车动画以及角色 IK 实现车门和方向盘的操作。

多人游戏：整个系统均采用复制技术，支持响应式客户端驾驶，无输入延迟或抖动，并提供监听模式与专用服务器支持。

损坏与变形：碰撞会导致车身变形、车漆受损、部件脱落以及玻璃碎裂。

轮胎、火灾与爆炸：车辆支持穿刺、起火和爆炸，可配置发动机及油箱的损伤区域。

角色软体人偶：碰撞时可碾压角色并弹出乘员，支持可配置的安全带设置。

原生混沌车辆物理引擎：由虚幻引擎原生的混沌车辆系统驱动，兼容标准UE设置、调校及官方文档。

地形适配：操控、声音与效果会根据地形变化而调整。

车辆基础功能：配备工作灯光、喇叭、方向盘以及速度/转速仪表盘，同时提供内外摄像头，支持外景的轨道与缩放控制。

自定义与特效：可调整车漆、污垢与划痕，包含驾驶音效、表面特效、刮擦、刹车发热及回火等。

修复与重生：可修复受损车辆，或重新生成以继续游戏。

基于距离的优化：裁剪可减少车辆逻辑、动画、特效和音频处理中的冗余内容。

✅ Key Features
Full character interaction: Drivers and passengers use adaptive entry/exit animations and character IK for doors and steering.

Multiplayer: The entire system is replicated, with responsive client driving, no input lag or jitter, and listen/dedicated server support.

Damage & deformation: Collisions deform bodywork, damage paint, detach parts and shatter glass.

Tires, fire & explosions: Vehicles support punctures, fire and explosions, with configurable engine and fuel-tank damage areas.

Character ragdolls: Run over characters and eject occupants in collisions, with a configurable seatbelt setting.

Native Chaos Vehicle physics: Powered by Unreal Engine’s native Chaos Vehicles system, with standard UE setup, tuning and official documentation fully applicable.

Surface adaptation: Handling, sounds and effects respond to the terrain.

Vehicle essentials: Working lights, horn, steering wheel and speed/RPM dashboard, plus interior and exterior cameras with orbit and zoom controls for the exterior view.

Customization & effects: Adjust paint, dirt and scratches, with driving audio, surface effects, scraping, brake heating and backfire.

Restoration & respawn: Restore damaged vehicles or respawn them for continued gameplay.

Distance-based optimization: Culling reduces unnecessary vehicle logic, animation, effects and audio processing.
